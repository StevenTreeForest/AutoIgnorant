Angels of Death

I went in knowing nothing. What began as suspicion about game-like dialogue became a strange romance, an impossible promise, and—somehow—a good adaptation.

6 moments Read top to bottom Spoiler-light

The short version

At first, the dialogue feels too much like it came straight from a video game. Then the central promise between a suicidal girl and a serial killer turns unexpectedly intimate. By the end, staying faithful to the original story feels like exactly the right decision.
